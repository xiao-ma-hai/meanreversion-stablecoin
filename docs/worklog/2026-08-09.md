# 2026-08-09 full-model implementation log

## Scope

- Removed the final-report complexity screen after the legacy MVP gate.
- Froze a 25-object registry covering every finite-dimensional article model
  with a constructible observation density and every benchmark/margin/copula
  family required by the empirical methodology.
- Required every registry object to end as estimated, weakly identified,
  constraint-failed, monotonicity/support-failed, or not observable. No silent
  omission is treated as completion.

## Implementation

- Added nonlinear OU/CIR transformations: exponential OU, quadratic CIR,
  exponential CIR, and explicit diagnostics for the OU/CIR special-function
  branches.
- Added Normal, Student-t, Fernandez--Steel skew Student-t, GED, and monotone
  PCHIP quantile margins.
- Added Gaussian, Student-t, Frank, Clayton, survival Clayton, Gumbel, and
  survival Gumbel copulas, each in base and independence-mixed form.
- Added seasonal deterministic-intensity MOU, jump OU, nested OU, two-state
  Markov-switching OU, a no-reversion random walk, finite-dimensional
  time-varying Normal-margin/Gaussian-copula dynamics, and the article's
  Gaussian--Gamma cases.
- Retained the mixed-copula bond PDE as not observable from the available spot
  series because neither a bond-price cross section nor a risk-neutral measure
  is present.

## Numerical corrections

- Cached Student-t probability transforms inside the dependence optimization;
  this preserves the likelihood and reduced a full four-horizon Student-t
  copula fit from more than one hour to tens of seconds.
- Replaced the rounded Frank Kendall-tau inversion with a continuous dense
  monotone interpolation, eliminating artificial zero numerical gradients.
- Vectorized Markov-switching emission densities and used a stable scalar HMM
  recursion. Also replaced nonsmooth kappa sorting by
  `kappa_2 = kappa_1 + exp(beta_2)`, preventing kappa/sigma regime mismatch.
- Replaced the Markov-switching primary validation approximation with the exact
  one-step filtered predictive density, resetting the state filter at timestamp
  gaps.
- Refined the Student-t copula degrees-of-freedom profile down to 2.05 and
  retained a boundary/weak-identification flag when the optimum is at the grid
  endpoint.

## Final empirical result

- Full-model stage gate: PASS (25/25 registry objects covered; 14 copula
  specifications; 5 margins; 960 period-horizon-margin-copula score cells).
- 2024 five-minute validation winner: MarkovSwitchingOU, mean conditional log
  score 8.82468584.
- Runner-up: Student-t margin + Student-t copula, 8.78120208.
- Paired difference: 0.04348376; 2,000-draw day-block 95% interval
  [0.01824155, 0.06595371].
- MarkovSwitchingOU remains weakly identified because its slow regime is near a
  parameter boundary and spans less than one mean-reversion unit in the longest
  contiguous estimation segment. It is a predictive winner, not a reliable
  structural-regime estimate.
- With a Student-t margin, the mixed reset improves the Gaussian copula score by
  0.07834728 and the two-scale Gaussian score by 0.04843167; it is effectively
  redundant for the Student-t copula and materially worsens the fitted
  Archimedean mixed variants. The mixed-copula advantage is family-specific.

## Theory derivation audit

- Added `theoretical_derivation_audit.tex`, covering every definition,
  proposition, OU/CIR transformation, Gamma construction, mixed-copula kernel,
  Poisson reset representation, and bond PDE stated in
  `meanreversion_article_main.tex`.
- Verified zero symbolic residuals for affine OU, exponential OU, affine CIR,
  quadratic CIR, and exponential CIR coefficient solutions.
- Derived the exact quadratic-CIR monotonicity condition. For standard `k>0`,
  global monotonicity fails at a finite time for every finite `B0`; for `k<0`,
  the map can be globally monotone iff
  `min(B0, B0 + A0*(sigma^2+2*k*theta)/k) >= 0`, but the base is no longer the
  article's standard mean-reverting CIR.
- Retained a symbolic counterexample showing that the article's second CIR
  change of variables does not reduce the original PDE to `u_tau=z*u_zz`.
- Classified the Gaussian--Gamma conditions as necessary point conditions, not
  proved global sign conditions; classified the CIR Kummer family as
  under-specified rather than mathematically impossible.
- Reproducible outputs:
  `output/tables/theory_derivation_audit.csv` and
  `output/models/theory_symbolic_checks.json`.
